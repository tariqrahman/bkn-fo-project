# Post-Randle Trade – Capped Out | 1 Open Spot | Randle Trade Restriction

After the early-July four-team trade, BKN added Julius Randle and Joshua Jefferson and moved Nicolas Claxton and Isaiah Evans. The roster is essentially at the cap with one open standard roster spot remaining.

BKN is no longer operating as a cap-space team this summer. The front office is likely focused on fit around Randle and MPJ, second-apron constraints, and short-term roster completion rather than clearing major room.

Randle cannot be traded until September 10, 2026 because of trade-restriction rules on his incoming contract.

BKN still does not control its 2027 first-round pick, which continues to influence how aggressively BKN tries to compete in the near term versus preserving flexibility.

Remaining paths discussed internally:

- Fill the last roster spot with a minimum or partial-taxpayer exception signing,
- Keep powder dry and carry the open spot into the season for trade flexibility,
- Use Terance Mann or Noah Clowney as outgoing salary in a smaller matching deal once Randle becomes tradable.
