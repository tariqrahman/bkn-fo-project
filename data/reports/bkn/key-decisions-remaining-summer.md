# Key Decisions – Remaining Summer

## MPJ

Keep, Extend, or Trade: MPJ remains extension-eligible for up to a four-year deal this summer. With Randle now in the frontcourt, BKN may view MPJ as a long-term co-star or as the best remaining veteran trade chip if another upgrade becomes available. An extension would reduce future trade flexibility but stabilize the scoring frontcourt.

## Clowney

Rookie Scale Extension: Clowney remains eligible for a rookie scale extension. Delaying a new deal preserves optionality because his projected cap hold gives BKN flexibility in future summers. Clowney can still be included as a young asset in a trade package if BKN pivots back toward a larger move after Randle's trade restriction lifts.

## Mann

Expiring Matching Salary: Mann is still one of BKN's most useful tradable contracts for salary-matching. He is more likely to be moved in a secondary deal than as part of a star hunt now that Claxton has already been dealt.

## Young Core

Brown Jr., Demin, and Powell: BKN must decide how much rotation responsibility to give the 2026 draft class this season versus development minutes in G League or bench roles. That choice affects whether BKN uses the final roster spot on a veteran or a young player.
